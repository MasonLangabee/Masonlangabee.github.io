Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Clip Poster requests only the permissions needed to read your basic profile (name and avatar) and upload videos to your account. It stores an access token so it can post on your behalf, and it does not collect, sell, or share any other personal data.

No data is shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time in TikTok under Settings > Security & permissions > Manage app permissions, after which the stored token stops working and is deleted.
